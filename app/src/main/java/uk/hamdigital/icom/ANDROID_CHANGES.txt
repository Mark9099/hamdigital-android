FT8CN's Icom network protocol (https://github.com/N0BOY/FT8CN, ft8cn/app/src/main/java/com/bg7yoz/ft8cn/icom, commit
a98fbfa), MIT licence (LICENSE-FT8CN), (C) 2023 BG7YOZ. Made by tools/port_icom.py; the protocol code is unchanged.

Copied: AudioUdp, ControlUdp, IComPacketTypes, IComWifiRig, IcomAudioUdp, IcomCivUdp, IcomControlUdp, IcomSeqBuffer,
IcomUdpBase, IcomUdpClient, WifiRig (package com.bg7yoz.ft8cn.icom -> uk.hamdigital.icom). Not copied: the Xiegu classes.

Changed (FT8CN's own app classes are not part of this app):
  - IcomUdpBase.getUdpStyle: English stream names instead of FT8CN's string resources.
  - IcomAudioUdp: no extra transmit-volume scaling (FT8CN's GeneralVariables.volumePercent; the app sets its level).
  - IcomCivUdp.sendPttAction: the CI-V PTT frame (FE FE civ E0 1C 00 01/00 FD) built in place of FT8CN's IcomRigConstant.
  - WifiRig: an OnStatus callback; IComWifiRig reports login and network errors through it instead of FT8CN's toasts,
    and does not open the phone-speaker playback (openAudio): the radio's audio goes to the app's decoders.
  - An unused checkerframework import removed.
The app's side is uk.hamdigital.rig.IcomNet (connect / CI-V / PTT / audio) with Ic705, AudioIn and Transmitter.

Fixed after the copy (0.8.5; found on the air - the radio kept a stale session after the app closed, so reopening the
app gave no login, or a login with no CI-V, for a minute or two):
  - IcomUdpClient.sendData: each packet is copied into its own SendDataRunnable and sent by a single-thread executor,
    in order. FT8CN reused one shared runnable on a cached pool and overwrote its data before it ran, so packets sent
    close together could be lost or duplicated.
  - IcomUdpClient.setActivated(false): waits (up to 0.5 s) for the send queue to empty before turning off and closing
    the socket. FT8CN closed it at once, so the disconnect / logout packets just queued were never sent ("Socket is
    closed").
  - IcomCivUdp.close: sends the CI-V open/close "close" before closing its socket (FT8CN sent it after).
  - ControlUdp.closeAll: closes the CI-V and audio streams first, then deletes the token and disconnects the control
    stream (as wfview does); FT8CN closed the control socket first and sent the CI-V close after the CI-V socket was
    shut.

Added (0.11.0, for FreeDV voice): IcomAudioUdp.pushTxAudio / startTxStream / stopTxStream - a queue of 12 kHz transmit
samples and a thread that sends one 20 ms packet (TX_BUFFER_SIZE samples, packed as sendTxAudioData packs them) every
20 ms while PTT is on, paced to the clock, silence when the queue is empty. FT8CN's sendTxAudioData sends one finished
recording at a time and cannot take speech as it is made. sendTxAudioData itself is unchanged.

Added (0.12.0, for FreeDV RADE): IcomAudioUdp.txQueued - how many samples are still queued, so the app can wait for the
end of an over (RADE's end-of-over frame, with the callsign) to go out before it stops the stream and drops PTT.
